## HCO⁺ Opacity Explorer

An interactive tool for exploring what the HCO⁺ / H¹³CO⁺ (1–0) line ratio can tell us about optical depth. Adjust the optical depth and abundance ratio to see how the simulated spectra change and where the line ratio stops constraining optical depth reliably.
