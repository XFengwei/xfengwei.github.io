I served as a hands-on tutor at the first China Astrochemistry School, supporting participants in two practical sessions.

The first tutorial introduced the FITS data format and covered how to read, write, inspect, and process astronomical data. The second focused on RADEX spectral fitting, guiding participants through the interpretation and modeling of molecular-line observations.
