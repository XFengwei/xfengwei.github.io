Presented in the Radio Astronomy session of the 2023 Chinese Astronomical Society Annual Meeting, this talk connected the early-stage protoclusters surveyed by ASHES with the more evolved systems studied by ASSEMBLE.

The comparison offers a dynamic view of massive-protocluster evolution, highlighting how dense cores grow, protoclusters contract, and mass segregation emerges as gas continues to assemble into stars and stellar clusters.
