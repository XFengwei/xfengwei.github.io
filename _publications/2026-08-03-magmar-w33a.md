ALMA 1.2-mm full-polarization observations resolve 20 dense cores and nine filaments in W33 A. The field morphology and energy balance show distinct roles for magnetic fields across the protocluster, including filament stabilization, magnetically regulated accretion, and delayed core collapse. An [arXiv preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.16915) is also available.
