The survey maps the 20 km s<sup>-1</sup> cloud, Sgr C, and dust-ridge cloud e at 1.3 and 3 mm with comparable 0.2-0.3 arcsec resolution. The widespread low spectral indices motivate new tests of compact optically thick structures, dust growth, and free-free emission in the Galactic Center.
